# Customer Churn Prediction and Retention Analytics

## Project Overview

This project uses data analytics and machine learning to understand customer churn and identify customers who may be at risk of leaving a telecommunications service provider.

The project aims to support data-driven customer retention strategies.

## Problem Statement

Customer churn can affect business revenue and long-term customer relationships. Businesses need to understand churn patterns and identify customers who may be likely to leave so they can plan appropriate retention activities.

## Objectives

* Analyze customer churn patterns.
* Identify important factors associated with churn.
* Explore customer characteristics and service usage.
* Build and compare machine learning models for churn prediction.
* Identify potentially at-risk customers.
* Recommend data-driven customer retention strategies.

## Dataset

The project uses the Telco Customer Churn dataset, which contains customer demographics, account information, subscribed services, billing details, tenure, and churn status.

## Machine Learning Models

The following models were evaluated:

* Logistic Regression
* Balanced Logistic Regression
* Decision Tree
* Random Forest

## Key Results

* Logistic Regression achieved 80.70% accuracy.
* Decision Tree achieved 80.75% recall for churn customers.
* The analysis identified higher churn rates among month-to-month customers and customers with shorter tenure.

## Business Recommendations

The project recommends improving early customer engagement, reviewing pricing and service value, offering suitable retention incentives, and using churn predictions to prioritize customer follow-ups.

## Limitations

Model predictions are not always correct. The results are based on the available dataset and should be validated before being used for real-world business decisions.
